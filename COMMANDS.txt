Any block that starts with "cd ~/Developer/game-mastery" runs in your own
terminal or in the Claude session opened at the repo root. Never paste it
into a worktree's Claude session: that session is sandboxed to its own
folder and cannot reach the root checkout, so it will refuse and hand it
back. In Claude Code, a ! in front of a line runs it in your shell instead.


Finish the worktree you are in (commit, rebase, guards, merge, push, catch up the rest).
Run from your own terminal, or with a ! in front in Claude Code. Change the message.

~/Developer/game-mastery/finish-worktree.sh "Sessions: what changed"


Catch up every worktree with main, without finishing anything.
